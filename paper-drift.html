Think one move ahead.

Each boat sails straight along its pointed end. When one leaves the lagoon, the current shifts the remaining boats and changes direction. A path you've just opened may become a new puzzle.

Earn light drops as you deliver letters, collect your daily mail and add paper lanterns, lotuses and wind chimes to your dock. Every decoration has its own movement and touch sound.

This build saves progress on your device. There is no cloud sync or developer-operated save recovery. Check the support information before deleting the app or changing devices.

Paper Drift, in a few words.

An offline paper-boat puzzle for iOS. Send each boat out along its pointed direction, then plan around currents that shift the remaining boats. The current development build includes 1,000 levels, a dock to decorate and daily rewards, with no account, live ads or paid purchases. There is no App Store download link yet.
