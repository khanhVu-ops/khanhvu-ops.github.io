Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Khanh Vu ("KV Apps", "we", "us", or "our") handles and protects your information across our iOS applications.

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

We believe your personal data belongs to you. Our applications are designed with privacy-by-architecture principles:

  • No account registration is required to use our applications.
  • Your sensitive files, notes, vaults, and photos are encrypted and stored locally on your device.
  • We do not host or operate backend servers to inspect, collect, or sell your personal content.

2. Information We Collect and Process

Depending on how you use our app, the following data may be processed:

  • On-Device Local Data: Any files, photos, or text you create within the app remain exclusively in the app's secure sandbox on your iOS device.
  • Biometrics / Authentication: If you secure the app using Face ID, Touch ID, or Passcode, authentication is handled natively by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. We never have access to your biometric samples or raw passcode.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

We may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support the development and maintenance of our free apps.

To serve relevant ads or monitor ad delivery performance, Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect technical diagnostic data, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA, subject to your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt).
  • IP address, approximate location, device manufacturer, model, and OS version.
  • Ad interaction statistics and crash diagnostics.

If you choose to opt out of tracking via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, you will only receive contextual, non-personalized advertisements.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your content is stored solely on your physical device:

  • Deleting the application from your iOS device will permanently remove all associated local data and vaults.
  • You can also delete individual files or clear app data anytime inside the app settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated revision date.
